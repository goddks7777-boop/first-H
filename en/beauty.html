Questions to ask before visiting the hospitalCheck before treatment

Q1. Can I discuss facial lines and skin concerns together?

yes. We look at areas of concern such as facial contour, left and right balance, and skin texture, tone, and elasticity. Talking about the concerns that concern you most will help us prioritize your care.

Q2. How are thread lifting and facial acupuncture different?

thread lifting is a procedure where threads are inserted into the tissue, and Jeongan Acupuncture is a method of placing needles on the facial area. We will examine your problem area and skin condition and explain which method can be applied.

Q3. Can I return to daily activities immediately after thread lifting?

It varies depending on the treatment area and scope. Bruising, swelling, and pain may occur after insertion, so if you have any important schedules, please let us know during your consultation. We will also inform you of when you can wash your face, apply makeup, and exercise.

Q4. Is it possible to maintain your body shape even after losing weight?

yes. If you have areas of concern or decreased skin elasticity even after weight loss, you can consult. We look at recent changes in weight and areas of concern and plan the necessary care first.

Q5. If I get an herbal beauty treatment, do I have to take herbal medicine as well?

Herbal medicine is considered when necessary depending on the current physical condition and purpose of treatment. After checking your usual eating, digestion, and sleeping conditions and the medications you are taking, we will consult with you about whether or not you should take herbal medicine.
