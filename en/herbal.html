Questions to ask before visiting the hospitalCheck before treatment

Q1. Is the prescription determined based solely on pulse diagnosis?

We examine symptoms and lifestyle habits through a medical examination, and review the findings from pulse examination and ultrasound. We will determine your prescription after checking your medical history and the medications you are currently taking.

Q2. I am taking other medications. Can I get counseling?

yes. Please tell us what prescription drugs, over-the-counter drugs, and health functional foods you are taking during your treatment. If you don't remember the name of the medication, show them a photo of your prescription or medication envelope. Check precautions when taking with herbal medicine.

Q3. How long should I take herbal medicine?

The duration of taking it depends on the purpose of prescription and your current physical condition. At the first treatment, we will guide you through the medication plan, and then adjust the period based on changes in your body and discomfort.

Q4. Can anyone take deer antler herbal medicine?

Decide whether to use deer antler by considering your current physical condition, purpose of taking it, medical history, and medications you are taking. If deer antler is suitable, the medicine to be prescribed together with it and how to take it will be explained.

Q5. How do I choose an individualized herbal medicine and Gongjindan?

Individualized herbal medicine is a method of determining the medicinal ingredients and dosage according to the results of examination, while gongjindan is an herbal medicine prescription with basic medicinal ingredients. At the clinic, we first listen to what inconveniences you are considering taking the drug and guide you to a prescription that suits your current physical condition.
