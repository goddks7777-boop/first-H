Privacy Policy

This is a guide to safely handling valuable personal information.

01. Purpose and items of personal information processing

Jeil Korean Medicine Clinic may process personal information necessary in the process of performing treatment and consultation services. There are no membership registration, consultation, or reservation forms on the website to directly enter personal information.

Personal information processed during phone consultations or medical treatment is subject to instructions and relevant laws and regulations at the time of collection.

02. Retention and destruction of personal information

Personal information is retained for the period necessary for the purpose of collection and use or as prescribed by relevant laws and regulations. Personal information that has expired or whose processing purpose has been achieved is destroyed, except in cases where it is required to be preserved in accordance with law.

Electronic files are deleted to make them difficult to recover, and paper documents are destroyed by shredding or incineration.

03. Use of external services

When you go to Naver Map or Search on the homepage, the privacy policy of that service applies. The homepage connects to Google Fonts to display fonts, and during the connection process, access information such as the user's IP address may be transmitted to the service provider.

04. Rights of information subjects and how to exercise them

The information subject and legal representative may request to view, correct, delete, or suspend processing of personal information in accordance with relevant laws and regulations. If you make a request to the contact information below, we will process it according to the procedures prescribed by law after verifying your identity. The exercise of rights may be restricted by law.
