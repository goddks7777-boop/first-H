Jeil Korean Medicine Clinic treatment tools and equipment

Where and how you apply it is as important as which tool you use.
Jeil Korean Medicine Clinic determines the necessary tools and application methods by examining the condition of the treatment area and tissue.

Mono & Cog Threads

These treatments use absorbable medical threads. Mono threads are used for skin elasticity, while barbed cog threads are used to support sagging tissue.

Acupuncture · Acupotomy · Electroacupuncture · Pharmacopuncture

Acupuncture stimulates acupuncture points, while acupotomy addresses adhesions in soft tissue. Electroacupuncture adds electrical stimulation, and pharmacopuncture delivers a medicinal solution.

CO2 laser

The thermal energy of a laser is used to remove skin lesions such as moles and warts. We determine the scope of treatment by checking the characteristics of the lesion and provide guidance on subsequent skin care methods.

ICT/TENS

It is a physical therapy device that delivers electrical stimulation through electrodes attached to the skin. It is used to relieve pain, and the intensity is adjusted according to the treatment area and response to stimulation.

chiropractic table

This is a treatment table used to support the body and position the body during manual therapy such as Chuna. Helps apply manual stimulation to muscles and joints in positions appropriate for the treatment area.

Gongjindan

This pill is prescribed after examining physical conditions such as fatigue and low energy. We check your sleep and digestion status as well as the medications you are taking and advise you on whether and how to take them.
